## Community history and company logos

Retrieved October 24, 2026. The company names and six crew members come from the existing [live homepage](https://genaimeetup.com/) and its original repository version (`0f034ef`). That homepage groups the companies as sponsors and speakers without assigning individual roles; their appearance here does not claim a current sponsorship. It lists both Sentient Foundation and Sentient AI, so both names are retained.

Logos are authentic company artwork, saved locally to avoid third-party image-loading dependencies and shown in their native colors. Header SVGs were extracted from the named official homepages; SVG namespaces and inherited colors were made self-contained where necessary, without redrawing the logos. Langflow uses its official colored symbol with black lettering; only its white canvas and excess viewBox whitespace were removed. Martian's excess viewBox whitespace was trimmed. The Snell & Wilmer wordmark uses the official navy (`#1D2437`) found in the compact header logo on the same official homepage, instead of the full header's reversed white lettering, for visibility on light backgrounds. Trademark ownership remains with the respective companies.
